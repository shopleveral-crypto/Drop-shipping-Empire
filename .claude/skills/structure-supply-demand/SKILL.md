# 3-step structure + supply/demand strategy (TradingLab)

Source: "The Only Trading Strategy You'll Ever Need", TradingLab, YouTube `e-QmGJU1XYc`, 8:38.
Learned from the English captions; the chart visuals were not available.

The video names no market or time frame; it presents the method as universal. It claims thousands of backtests with every tested month profitable, but shows no statistics. The examples shown are winners only.

## Step 1 — Market structure, using the "valid low" rule
- Uptrend = higher highs + higher lows. Downtrend = lower lows + lower highs.
- **Core rule: a low is only *valid* if the move up from it broke the previous high.** A valid high is the mirror: the move down from it broke the previous low.
- **The trend changes only when price breaks the last *valid* low (or high).**
  - In an uptrend, a break of some intermediate low that never produced a new high is **not** a reversal. The trend is still bullish.
  - Price can do anything (up, down, sideways) as long as it doesn't break the valid low.
- When price breaks above the previous high, the valid low **moves up** to the low that produced that break.
- The video's most common mistake: calling a downtrend on a break of a non-valid low.
- "If you get this step slightly wrong, the whole strategy fails."

## Step 2 — Supply and demand zones
- **Only trade with the trend.** Uptrend: look only for demand zones and longs. Downtrend: look only for supply zones and shorts. Shorting an uptrend is "just silly".
- **Demand zone:** an area of consolidation (sideways price) followed by a sharp impulsive move up.
  - Mark it from the **low to the high of the last candle before the impulse move**.
- **Supply zone:** the mirror. Use the last candle before a sharp impulsive move down.
- **The logic:** the big move shows many traders wanted to buy (or sell) there, so they will likely do it again when price returns.
- **Entry:** when price comes back into the zone.
- **Stop:** just beyond the zone (below demand, above supply).
- **Take profit:** the recent high (longs) or recent low (shorts).
- After a zone plays out, keep repeating: every new zone created while the trend holds is a new setup.

## Step 3 — Risk:reward filter
- Take the trade **only if R:R ≥ 2.5:1** (stop beyond the zone, target at the recent swing).
- Even if steps 1 and 2 are met, skip anything under 2.5.
- The author says this single rule improves the strategy's profitability "by a ton".

## Checklist
1. Is the trend up or down by valid-high/low structure?
2. Is there a zone in the trend direction: the last candle before an impulsive move?
3. Has price returned into the zone?
4. Is the stop beyond the zone and the target at the recent swing, giving R:R ≥ 2.5?
5. Then enter, set the stop and target, and let it play out.

## Gaps to define before backtesting
The video leaves these open:
- What counts as a swing high/low: number of bars, wick vs body.
- What counts as an "impulsive" move: e.g. a candle range ≥ X × ATR.
- What counts as "consolidation" before it.
- Whether a zone is used once only.
- Entry at the first touch of the zone edge vs the middle.
- Time frame and market.

Pick explicit values and say which ones were chosen when reporting results.
